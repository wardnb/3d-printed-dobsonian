Ordering notes

  • Plate type: ATP-5 or MIC-6 cast plate suits the rings, the focuser board and the cell frame, since it stays flat when the slots and pockets come out. Rolled 6061-T6 works everywhere. Stiffness is the same.
  • Circles beat rings and squares on price at Midwest (1/4″ cast: $144.54 per 21¼″ disc against $170.74 per 21½″ square), so option B is the default. Their standard cut tolerance is −0.00″/+0.125″, so a 21″ disc is safe: it leaves at least 0.25″ round the ring. Hold it with screws through the centre waste and cut the ring's OD last.
  • Rings (option C): if you ever buy rings, ask for at least 21″ OD and at most 17.25″ ID and machine both edges to 20.50″ / 17.50″.
  • Spare discs: extra circles from a quantity break are useful stock: the second 1/4″ disc's 17″ centre and any extra 1/2″ disc can hold later brackets (alt drive arm, read-head mounts, electronics plate).
  • 1/2″ plate: for the cell frame, put steel thread inserts in the three 5/16-24 bolt holes if the frame is cast plate.
  • Bed: everything fits the AltMill bed (48.6 × 24.4″) as a single fixture.
