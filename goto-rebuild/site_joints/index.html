Drawn along the pole axis. The pole hangs from the cage at the top and seats in the socket block at the bottom. Scale in inches.

Why it holds collimation

A pin in a hole always has clearance, and the truss analysis puts the whole error budget in that clearance: a 5,000 lb/in joint drifts a millimetre from horizon to zenith. A cone in a cup has no clearance by construction. Gravity seats it, it self-centres, and the load spreads over the whole conical face. The knob adds no stiffness and its torque does not matter in the dark.
