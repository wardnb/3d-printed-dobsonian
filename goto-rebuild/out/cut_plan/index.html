Ordering notes

  • Plate type: ATP-5 or MIC-6 cast plate suits the rings, the focuser board and the cell frame, since it stays flat when the slots and pockets come out. Rolled 6061-T6 works everywhere. Stiffness is the same.
  • Rings (option C): ask for at least 21″ OD and at most 17.25″ ID, and machine both edges to 20.50″ / 17.50″. Ring and circle cuts come with loose saw tolerances, so never order them at finished size.
  • Circles (option B): a 21¼″ disc leaves about 0.37″ round the ring. Hold it with screws through the centre waste, not the rim.
  • 1/2″ plate: for the cell frame, put steel thread inserts in the three 5/16-24 bolt holes if the frame is cast plate.
  • Bed: everything fits the AltMill bed (48.6 × 24.4″) as a single fixture.
